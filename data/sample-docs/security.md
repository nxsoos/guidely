# Security handbook

## Account protection
Enable multi-factor authentication on all company accounts. Never share passwords, one-time codes, or recovery codes. Use the approved password manager to create unique passwords.

## Suspicious messages
Report suspicious emails using the Report Phishing button in your email application. Do not click links or open attachments in a suspicious message. If you already clicked a link, contact urgent IT support immediately.

## Lost devices
Report a lost or stolen company device to urgent IT support within one hour of discovering the loss. Include the device type and last known location. IT will lock the device remotely where possible.

## Customer data
Store customer data only in approved company systems. Do not upload customer data to personal cloud storage or send it to personal email. Share access only with colleagues who need it for their work.
