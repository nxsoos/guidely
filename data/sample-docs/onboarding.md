# New employee onboarding

## Before your first day
People Operations sends a welcome email three working days before your start date. Complete the personal details form and upload proof of identity through the secure People Portal.

## Your first morning
Arrive at reception at 9:00 AM on your first day. Your onboarding buddy will meet you and help collect your badge and laptop. Remote employees receive a video meeting invitation instead.

## Required training
Complete security awareness and data privacy training within your first seven calendar days. The Learning Hub tracks completion. Your manager checks your training progress.

## First month
Schedule a 30-day check-in with your manager. Discuss role expectations, progress, and any support you need. Your onboarding buddy remains available throughout your first month.
