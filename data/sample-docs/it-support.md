# IT support guide

## Contacting support
Submit routine IT requests through the Service Desk portal. Support is available Sunday through Thursday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, company local time. Include your device name and a description of the problem.

## Password recovery
To reset your password, choose Forgot password on the company sign-in page. Verify your identity using your registered authenticator app. If you cannot access your authenticator, contact the Service Desk for identity verification.

## Laptop requests
Request a new laptop through Service Desk under Equipment Requests. Your line manager must approve the request. IT normally provisions approved laptops within five working days.

## Urgent incidents
For a suspected security breach or a company-wide outage, call the urgent IT number listed in the Service Desk portal. Do not send passwords or recovery codes in support tickets.
