# Expense reimbursement

## Submitting a claim
Submit business expense claims in the Finance Portal within 30 calendar days of purchase. Attach an itemized receipt and a short explanation of the business purpose. Your manager approves the claim before Finance reviews it.

## Meals
Business travel meals may be reimbursed up to 150 SAR per person per day. Alcohol and personal entertainment are not reimbursable. The meal limit includes taxes and tips.

## Travel approval
Get written manager approval before booking flights or hotels. Use economy class for flights. Book accommodation through the approved travel provider listed in the Finance Portal.

## Payment schedule
Finance pays approved claims in the next monthly payroll, provided Finance approval is complete by the 20th of the month. Later approvals are paid in the following payroll.
