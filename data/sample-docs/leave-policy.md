# Leave policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees at the fictional Cedarworks company receive 25 paid working days of annual leave each calendar year. Public holidays and weekends do not use annual leave days.

## Requesting time off
Submit an annual leave request in the People Portal at least 10 working days before your planned leave. Your line manager approves or declines the request. Do not book non-refundable travel before approval.

## Sick leave
Notify your manager before 9:00 AM on the first day of sickness. Upload a medical certificate to the People Portal when you are absent for three or more consecutive working days.

## Carryover
You may carry over up to five unused annual leave days. Use carried-over days before March 31 of the following year; otherwise they expire.
